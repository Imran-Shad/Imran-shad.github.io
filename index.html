Introduction

I am Md. Imran Hossain Shad, working as a Graduate Research Assistant at the Nano Synthesis Lab, Department of Nanomaterials and Ceramic Engineering, Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). I am pursuing an M.Sc. in Nanomaterials and Ceramic Engineering at BUET, and I completed my B.Sc. in Materials Science and Engineering at Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (RUET) in 2023.

My current work focuses on fabricating metal-free nanocomposites as photocatalysts to degrade real-world pollutants, funded by the Committee for Advanced Studies and Research (CASR), BUET. Before joining BUET, I worked as a Research Assistant at the Bangladesh Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (BCSIR) from 2023 to 2024, and as an Undergraduate Research Assistant in the Surface Engineering Lab at RUET from 2022-23. My research interests include nanoparticle synthesis, nanocomposites, polymer, photocatalysis, doped metal-oxide semiconductors, materials characterization and mechanical testing.
