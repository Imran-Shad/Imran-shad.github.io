I am Md. Imran Hossain Shad, working as a Graduate Research Assistant in the Nano Synthesis Lab, Department of Nanomaterials and Ceramic Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). I am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Nanomaterials and Ceramic Engineering at BUET, and I completed my B.Sc. in Materials Science and Engineering at Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (RUET) in 2023.

Prior to my graduate studies, I worked as a Research Assistant at the Bangladesh Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (BCSIR) from 2023 to 2024, and as an Undergraduate Research Assistant in the Surface Engineering Lab at RUET from 2022 to 2023. My research interests include nanoparticle synthesis, nanocomposites, doped metal-oxide semiconductors, materials characterization, PFAS degradation, and photocatalysis for environmental remediation.
